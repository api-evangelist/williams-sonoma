Williams-Sonoma, Inc. is a Fortune 500 American publicly traded retail company specializing in kitchenware and home furnishings. It is one of the largest e-commerce retailers in the United States. The company operates multiple brands including Williams Sonoma, Pottery Barn, Pottery Barn Kids, PBteen, West Elm, Rejuvenation, Mark and Graham, and Green Row. Williams-Sonoma does not offer a public developer API. The company uses internal APIs and third-party platforms for e-commerce, registry services, and supply chain management.
